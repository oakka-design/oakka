time-to-sell-section:
  title: When it's time to sell
  text-box: |+
    If your property isn’t attracting interest, it’s often not about the building itself but how it’s being seen.

    I offer clear, practical guidance to help a space feel welcoming and well proportioned before it goes on the market. This may involve small layout changes, improving lighting, adjusting textiles or suggesting what to store or simplify.

    We work mainly with what you already have, making targeted changes where they will have the most impact.
